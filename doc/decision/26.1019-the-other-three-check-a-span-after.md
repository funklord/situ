# 26.1019: the other three check a span after

Status: recorded
Date: 2026-11-14
Source: 26.1016, whose run the other three builders had to refuse

**26.1016 gave C a span its expression sizes** -- RTP's padding, ID3's
frame content, QUIC's Initial payload -- and the other three were made to
refuse it, having been found writing it unchecked.

**Now they check it as C does.** After the closing view, the reader's own
accessor for the member must be as long as the caller's bytes, and a
BOUNDS from validation -- a stated length past the bytes, which the
reader's clamped accessor cannot see -- is reported as CONSTRAINT. Only for
a member at the struct's top level, where the accessor is the member's own
name; one inside an arm stays C's.

**And a fault the work found.** C++'s counted value array refused a null
pointer at a count of zero, as C's did before 26.1016: RTP's empty CSRC
list. It is refused now only where something would be read.

**Measured**: RTP's five cases from 26.1016 -- plain, padded, a last byte
of 2 and of 5 over three bytes, padding with P clear -- in Python, C++ and
Rust, the packets byte for byte and the three refused. The agreement test
builds QUIC's Initial in all four alike; its generic values do not satisfy
RTP's or ID3's arithmetic, so there it compares only refusals, and the RTP
cases above are what hold the three to C.
