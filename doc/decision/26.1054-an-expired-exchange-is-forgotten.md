# 26.1054: an expired exchange is forgotten

Status: recorded
Date: 2026-11-17
Source: reading C's driver for fuzznet's asks (inbox 20261117T1123Z)

**C's driver stopped sending after `cap` timeouts.** It keeps two
tables: its own slots, holding the bytes and the schedule, and the
conversation's, holding each request's key for matching the reply. On
expiry it dropped its own slot and left the key. So a late reply to the
expired exchange still matched -- where `_match` says such a reply is
SITU_ERR_CONSTRAINT -- and every expiry kept one conversation slot: with
two slots, two sends that both expired made the third `_send`
SITU_ERR_BOUNDS, and so it stayed. A send refused for want of a driver
slot left its key behind the same way. C++, Rust and Python keep the key
in the driver's own slot and were not affected.

## The rule

The conversation table can forget a request by its id, and the driver
does so wherever an exchange ends without its reply: on expiry, and when
`_send` is refused.

## Measured

`test_an_expired_exchange_is_forgotten.py`: two slots, two sends, both
expired at 200 ms; the third send is SITU_OK and a late reply to the
first is SITU_ERR_CONSTRAINT. Without the forget on expiry the third send
is SITU_ERR_BOUNDS.
