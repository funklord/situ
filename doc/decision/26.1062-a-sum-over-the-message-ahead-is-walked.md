# 26.1062: a sum over the message ahead is walked, and refused past the frame

Status: recorded
Date: 2026-11-17
Source: 26.1050's open half, "the walkers do not verify such a tag"; the
holder's word, 2026-11-17, to build what a protocol needs

**Two of the six readers read PCI VPD's RV as a plain byte.** 26.1050 let
a checksum cover every byte of the message ahead of it and gave it to the
four backends and the dissector. The image carried a tag's coverage as its
first and last covered member, `covers(before)` names none, so the packer
wrote no tag row and neither walker knew the member was a tag. situ-edit,
built on the Python walker, left RV stale after any write ahead of it. No
corpus schema held such a tag, so no sweep asked.

## The image

The tag row's sixth word, which was `prefix` as 0 or 1, is a flag word:
bit 0 a prefix the message does not hold (14.2a), bit 1 `covers(before)`.
Such a row carries `NONE` for its first and last member, and the member is
marked a tag. A reader from before this sees the word nonzero and
declines, which is the answer it should give.

## The readers

- **Python walker** (`report._sum`): the span is the message from its
  first byte up to the tag. A view's buffer is the message and its offset
  absolute in it, so a tag in a nested struct counts the bytes ahead of
  that struct too -- what the backends' view owner and the dissector's
  root tvb count.
- **C walker** (`situ_walk_sum`): the same span, for a struct acquired at
  the message's start.
- **situ-edit**: a write to a member placed ahead of the tag in the same
  struct recomputes it over the message up to the tag.
- **Differential**: a `before` tag is asked its range and, where all three
  of C, Rust and Python derive its codec, its sum; Rust is handed the
  message (`_covered_in(&raw)`, `_compute_in(&raw)`).

## Found: all four backends summed past the frame

The span's end is where the tag sits, and that offset is taken from
lengths the frame clamps. `90 09 01` declares nine bytes of data and has
one: the tag sits at offset 3, which is the frame's end, and all four
answered `covered 0+3` and a sum of 102 over bytes the message does not
say are there. They agreed with each other, so the differential could
not see it; the walkers refused. All four now refuse a span whose tag's
own bytes do not fit the view -- which is enough, since a clamped length
anywhere ahead puts the tag at the frame's end. It is 26.1058's fault
met at the other kind of coverage.

## Found: the unparser dropped the clause

`covers(before)` leaves the tag's region tuple empty, and the unparser
printed only the tuple, so the tag reparsed as one covering nothing. The
round trip over the corpus caught it the moment `edges` held one, and it
is a commit of its own ahead of this one.

## Measured

`test_a_walker_sums_the_message_ahead.py`, against sums worked by hand as
`-(bytes ahead) & 0xFF`: both walkers answer 107 for `90 02 01 02 6b` and
refuse `90 09 01`; the nested `ahead_held` sums the two bytes ahead of
its struct (37); situ-edit recomputes after `kind` is written; and the
four backends print the walkers' lines for both messages. Skewing either
walker's span by a byte, or widening Python's guard, fails it.

`edges.situ` carries `summed_ahead` and `ahead_held`; Rust's census row
for it reads 27, the two sites being the construct's (measured with the
two structs cut).
