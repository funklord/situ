# 26.1029: packed decimal is written

Status: recorded
Date: 2026-11-14
Source: the goal gap -- builder refusals over the example schemas

**An RTC's time could not be built.** `wall_clock` is a DS1307's seven
time registers, every value packed decimal -- "is packed and its value is
not its bits" for the seconds beside the halt bit, "is packed BCD, whose
value is not its bytes" for the year.

**Its value is its digits, a nibble each**, which is what the runtime's
`situ_bcd_encode` makes and the reader's `situ_bcd_decode` undoes -- the
same encoder the reader's own setter calls, so there is no second copy of
the conversion to disagree with the first (the fault `owned.py` records
against its own BCD). C's builder writes a plain BCD member, packed or a
whole byte, through it, refusing a value past what the field holds: all
nines, or less where the field is narrower than its digits (0027) -- seven
bits of seconds hold 79. A BCD element, arm or size field keeps the
refusal. C only: the other three refuse the part.

**Measured against the DS1307 datasheet's register map**: Saturday
2026-11-14 at 02:59:30 is `30 59 02 07 14 11 26`, the halt bit sets bit 7
of the seconds register (`B0`), seconds of 60 and of 80 are refused, and a
year of 99 is `99`. The claim "is packed and its value is not its bits"
now has no population -- what is left behind it is a packed text number,
and digits are whole bytes -- so its row in the refusal table goes.
