# 26.1096: a variant with a length of its own measures itself

Status: recorded
Date: 2026-11-18
Source: openmlx4, inbox 20261118T1943Z item 2 -- a coded region inside an
authenticated body switched the checksum off

**openmlx4 described MFA's flag-chosen xz as situ had advised (26.1040,
26.1068), and the archive's CRC32 stopped being checked.** `verify` said
"mfa_archive.crc: no codec computes it" and passed a vector with the CRC's
last byte flipped; the generated code had no `crc_check` in any of the
four backends. The same schema without the xz arm checked it.

The cause was not the codec and not the region. Each section's payload is
`variant payload[hdr.size]`, and `has_computable_extent` asked a variant of
its ARMS: xz's expansion is unbounded, so that arm had no length, the
section had no extent, nothing after the first section had a place, and the
CRC -- after all of them -- could not be located. A variant with a length
of its own is that many bytes whichever arm it holds; the arms decide what
the bytes mean, not how many there are. An `opaque` default under a length
is the same shape without a codec, and lost the member after its struct
the same way.

## The rule

A variant sized by a field or an expression is measurable whatever its
arms are; only an unsized variant is asked of its arms, as before.

## Measured

`test_a_sized_variant_measures_itself`: the MFA shape refuses a flipped
CRC through `verify` and all four backends emit `crc_check`; with the rule
reverted all five cases fail. `edges.after_opaque_arm` carries the opaque
shape through the four backends' builds and checks, both walkers and the
dissector.
