# 26.1098: an `at` through checksummed bytes carries no marker

Status: recorded
Date: 2026-11-18
Source: openmlx4, inbox 20261118T1943Z item 1 -- FS2's `gph_chain`
follows `section.hdr.next`, inside the CRC16's authenticated body, before
and regardless of the CRC

**openmlx4 asked for a way to SAY that a placement is read before its check
-- `following at [unverified] section.hdr.next`, or a region-level
"readable before its check" -- rather than for the `at` to be refused or
silently allowed.** What they wanted it for was the sentinel end and the
"list goes forward" rule, which they had held back believing situ would not
steer a parse by bytes inside the CRC.

## What was measured

Neither waited on the CRC. The forward rule, `when
gph_chain.section.hdr.next <= offset(gph_chain) refuse ...`, builds in all
four backends today and openmlx4's seven FS2 vectors conform under it. The
sentinel, `variant rest switch (section.hdr.next)`, was refused by the
discriminant's DEPTH -- two levels into a nested struct, which a plain
struct refused as well -- and builds since 26.1097. One level into the
same authenticated body always built.

## Why no marker

Every read of a covered field precedes its check, in every reader situ
generates: `validate` computes no checksum (0051), and the caller runs
`crc_check` when it chooses. A length inside an AAD, a discriminant, and an
`at` are alike in this, so a word required on `at` alone would assert a
distinction the generated code does not have, and one on every such read
would mark every checksummed format throughout. Whether a reader checks
before it follows is the reader's order -- fs2.c follows first on purpose,
so that `verify` reports every damaged section -- and that is stated where
the order is chosen, in the caller.

What would change this: a generated accessor that verifies before it
places (built and set aside in 26.1046, because it changed what `validate`
means for one construct), or a peer contract that must record the order.
Either is a new decision, not this one.

The refusal 26.1046 holds back for `authenticated` stays held back: it
would refuse FS2's chain for a property every other read shares.
