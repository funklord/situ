# 26.869: The builders write a varint

Status: recorded -- 225 builders over 31 schemas, 98 of them an arm. Text
numbers, argued spans, wide runs and pads are what is left
Date: 2026-11-05
Source: 26.868's measure

A varint takes a 64-bit number, signed under `transform = zigzag`, and the
runtime's `put` writes the minimal encoding, which satisfies a `minimal`
declaration by construction. Rust's and Python's runtimes gain `varint_put` and
`varint_be_put`, line for line with C's. They had the read half only. C++ calls
C's through `situ.h`. A varint may be a size field, with no expression between
it and what it measures: solving one is declined.

`max_bits` bounds the value, and is checked before encoding. A be128 number too
wide for the format is refused rather than truncated.

`test_a_nested_member_is_one_whole_message.py` holds all four to published
vectors: 300 as `ac 02`, ZigZag's -1 as `01`, and a be128 300 as `82 2c`.
