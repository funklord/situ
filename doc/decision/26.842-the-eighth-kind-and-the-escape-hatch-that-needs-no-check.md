# 26.842: the eighth kind, and the escape hatch that needs no check

Status: recorded -- the enum kind ported, with `default = pass` getting
neither a check nor a decline
Date: 2026-11-03
Source: 26.838's prelude, the third consumer of it

## What it asks

An enum field admits its members and, with `default = error`, nothing
else. Section 8.7 says so and every backend emitted that as a comment
until three of them were compared.

**The value written is the lowest the member's own width can hold that
names no member** -- not the lowest byte. In `--target c` the range was
256 whatever the field held, beside a gate that skipped anything wider
than a byte, so `ethernet.ethertype`, `mqtt.packet.kind` and twenty-one
others declared their members and nobody checked that a value outside
them was refused. Where the enum covers its whole width there is nothing
to reject, and that is stated as a decline rather than dropped.

## `default = pass`: no check, and no decline either

This is the distinction worth recording, because the other kinds in this
file name everything they do not do.

Section 8.7 offers `default = pass` deliberately: IANA keeps assigning
numbers, and a receiver refusing tomorrow's would refuse tomorrow's
traffic. So an enum carrying it is **not** saying "this cannot be asked"
-- it is saying there is no such value. A decline would read as a gap in
the suite where the schema has made a choice.

`--target c` emitted the rejection check regardless of the policy at one
point, so a schema using the escape hatch failed a test asserting it had
not. That is the opposite error and the reason the policy is consulted
first.

**The rule the two cases make together**: name a decline when the suite
could have asked and did not; say nothing when the schema says there is
nothing to ask. A reader cannot tell those apart from a silence, and
cannot tell them apart from a decline either -- so the choice between
them is a claim about which it is.

## The control

Neutralising `known_enum` in the Python runtime -- the helper every
generated enum refusal calls -- **fails 28 of the 39 checks across 17 of
the corpus's schemas, and every one of the 28 is this kind.**

The 11 that stay green do not reach their refusal through that helper.
Said rather than rounded to "all", for the reason 26.840 gives: the reach
is 28, and that is the number the next sabotage is aimed with.

## The state

Eight of nine kinds in `--target python`. `passes_unknown` joined the six
constraint helpers in `situc/codegen`, both backends now asking it, proved
byte-identical over all 43 schemas' `--target c` output.

What remains is the framing reader, which needs none of the baseline
machinery, and the pinned-run body, which would widen the seventh kind by
the 18 byte-run pins it declines.
