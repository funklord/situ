# 26.840: the seventh kind, and the pin a number cannot break

Status: recorded -- `must_eq` enforced for the scalar form, with the 18
byte-run pins declined by name; the pinned-run body is what remains
Date: 2026-11-03
Source: 26.838's prelude, which this is the second consumer of

## What it asks

A pinned value, broken by **adding one inside the member's own width**,
must be refused on parse. Malleability control rather than pedantry, by
section 8.8's argument unchanged: a value the schema does not admit has to
be refused, or a sender varies bytes the format calls fixed.

Adding one wraps within the width, so the result differs from the pin at
every width above zero. That is why the break goes through `_break_to`
rather than writing a byte: `--target c` once wrote exactly one byte and
silently skipped every member that was not one byte wide, leaving
`bmp.signature`, `sqlite.magic` and `image_header.format_version` all
pinned and none of them tested.

## The control

**Making the emitter skip `must_eq` entirely fails 38 of the 40 checks
across 19 of the corpus's schemas, and every one of the 38 is this kind.**

The two that stay green are worth saying rather than rounding away: a pin
that another constraint also refuses does not need this check to notice,
so the kind's reach is 38 and not 40. A number stated as "all" is a number
nobody can use to aim the next sabotage.

And inside each check the baseline is its own control: `view.validate()`
must succeed over the prelude buffer before the pin is broken, or a refusal
afterwards could be about a buffer the schema already refused.

## What it declines, and why that is 18 and not a rounding error

49 of the corpus's 67 pins are a foldable scalar `[must_eq = ...]`. The
other **18 are byte RUNS**, and a number cannot break one: `"BM"` has
nothing to add one to, and a schema admitting several alternatives needs a
byte outside every one of them. That is `--target c`'s pinned-run body,
which is not ported.

They are declined by name, for the reason this file keeps restating:
declining 18 silently would leave a reader of 40 checks believing all 67
had been asked.

Two further declines a pin can hit: an offset the message chooses, so no
one buffer breaks it; and a `must_eq` that is not a compile-time value,
which cannot be folded and so cannot be broken.

## The state

Seven of nine kinds in `--target python`. What remains is the enum kind --
which has the prelude it needs and wants only the enum's own admitted set
-- and the framing reader, which needs none of this. The pinned-run body
widens this kind by 18 when it lands and is the larger of the two
remaining pieces.
