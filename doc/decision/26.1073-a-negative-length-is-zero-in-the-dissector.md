# 26.1073: a negative length is zero in the dissector, and an unreadable arm is unselected

Status: recorded
Date: 2026-11-18
Source: the dissector-walker differential, once the walker listed runs
inside an authenticated region (26.1070)

**The dissector showed TCP's last bytes as options when the data offset
lied.** `options[(data_offset - 5) * 4]` with a data offset of two is
`-12`, which reads as zero (14.2b) in all four backends and the walker. The
generated Lua took the count as it came: `tvb(at, -12)` is the rest of the
frame in Wireshark, so 22 bytes were shown as options, and `at = math.min(at
+ -12, ...)` walked the cursor back twelve, so `payload` was read over them
again. Nothing compared it until 26.1070, because the walker left every run
inside a covered region out of its listing.

**And the walker printed nothing for an arm it could not read** where every
driver answers `ok=0 value=0`: radiotap's arms, on any frame too short to
hold their selector, went uncompared.

## The rule

- Every count and length the dissector computes from the message is
  `math.max(0, ...)`: a negative one is zero, as in the other six readers.
- `_arm_values` answers `ok=0 value=0` for an arm whose selector or value
  the frame does not hold, as each driver does.
- A varint the frame cuts off is `len=0 value=0` in the walker, as in each
  driver: wasm's section sizes and cbor's heads went uncompared on every
  short frame.

## Measured

The dissector-walker differential failed on tcp over three draws
(`tcp_header.options#len: the dissector shows 22 and the walker reads 0`)
and passes with the clamp, with the rest of the dissector suite (508).
Over the corpus the lines C answers and the walker omits fell from 639 to
563 across 26.1069 to this one; of the `ok=` lines, from 58 to 20.
