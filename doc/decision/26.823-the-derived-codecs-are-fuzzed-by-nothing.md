# 26.823: the derived codecs are fuzzed by nothing

Status: recorded -- a measurement; whether `gen-fuzz` widens is the holder's
Date: 2026-11-03
Source: `project.md` section 26; sections 1 and 20.3

**Section 1 names "test scaffolding: golden-vector tests and a fuzz
harness per schema" as one of four things `situc` generates.** Walked
against the corpus: 43 of 46 schemas get a harness with at least one
dispatch arm, and three get one that fuzzes nothing.

| schema | arms | why |
|---|---|---|
| `example/register` | 0 | correct by design |
| `std/codecs.situ` | 0 | declares codecs, no struct |
| `std/kernels.situ` | 0 | declares codecs, no struct |

**`gen-fuzz`'s scope is stated and the first row is it working.** Its
docstring is "Generate a fuzz harness per parseable struct", its reason
is section 20.3 -- "parse safety is the top risk in a protocol parser" --
and it excludes a register deliberately, with the comment saying
`gen-dissector` had excluded them first. A register is a bus transaction
and not bytes off a wire.

So the other two rows are not a defect in `gen-fuzz` either. A schema
that declares no struct has no parser to fuzz, and the harness it gets is
honest about that: 46 lines, an `LLVMFuzzerTestOneInput` that returns 0.

## The finding is what that leaves unfuzzed

`std/kernels.situ` and `std/codecs.situ` are where the DERIVED codecs are
declared, and `situc gen-derived` emits **113 entry points** for
`kernels.situ` alone -- `situ_crc32`, `situ_crc32_spans`,
`situ_crc32_holed`, `situ_crc32_bits` and the same four per CRC variant,
plus Reed-Solomon, Hamming, the shift registers, bit stuffing and the
escape codes. That is generated code full of buffer arithmetic, and
nothing in the tree fuzzes any of it.

**The two worst memory faults this project has had were in exactly that
code, and neither was found by fuzzing:**

- **26.465**, a stack buffer over-read in the generated Reed-Solomon
  decoder, reachable from 64 attacker-chosen bytes and present since the
  decoder was written. Found by asking which runtime-computed index Rust
  could not prove in bounds -- and its own record says a fuzzer would
  have to GUESS A CODEWORD to reach it: 0 of 4000 random blocks trigger
  it, 300 of 300 codewords of RS(64,60) do.
- **26.482**, a heap-buffer-overflow WRITE in `situ_slip_decode`, from an
  unmodified corpus schema, with the buffer sized to exactly the capacity
  the generated guard computes. Found under ASan.

26.465 is the honest caveat on this whole finding: a fuzz harness over
Reed-Solomon would probably not have found 26.465 either. What it would
reach is the rest -- the CRC span and hole arithmetic, the stuffing
boundaries, the escape codes -- and 26.482 shows that family does produce
reachable writes.

## What a codec harness would do, and whose decision it is

- **The option.** `gen-fuzz` gains a dispatch arm per derived codec with
  a generated body: feed the input to `decode`, bound the output buffer
  from `expansion`, and where the codec declares `invertible`, round-trip
  and compare. Extern codecs have no body in situ and get nothing.
- **The cost.** It widens `gen-fuzz` from "per parseable struct" to "per
  parseable struct and per derived codec", which is a change to the
  module's stated purpose rather than a gap in it -- so section 20.3's
  framing ("parse safety is the top risk in a PROTOCOL PARSER") wants a
  sentence about codec bodies too. The emitter work is a family-by-family
  signature walk, the shape `codegen/kernel_program.py` already has.
- **Whose.** Yours. The module says what it is for, and widening a stated
  purpose is not a thing to do while passing through.

## Measured, and one correction to how

The count above took three attempts and the first two were my own
instrument:

1. Counting `LLVMFuzzerTestOneInput` occurrences said all 46 had a
   target. The symbol is there in all 46; in three of them the body is
   `(void)data; (void)size; return 0;`.
2. Searching the body for `situ_\w+(` said all 46 were vacuous. The real
   harnesses dispatch to `fuzz_<struct>` helpers, so the body names no
   `situ_` call at all -- `edges_fuzz.c` is 6370 lines and 111 arms.
3. Counting dispatch arms gave 43 and 3, which reading `kernels_fuzz.c`
   in full confirms.

**A count inherits its detector**, and the two wrong detectors each
produced a confident, plausible, wrong number in opposite directions.
What settled it was reading one file end to end.
