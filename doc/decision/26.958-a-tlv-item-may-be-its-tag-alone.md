# 26.958: a TLV item may be its tag alone

Status: recorded
Date: 2026-11-11
Source: a sweep of TLV shapes through all six readers
Supersedes: the zero-size refusal acb384e2 added to a `tlv` region's value sizes

**`value_size = switch (code) { case 0: 0, ... }` is accepted.** DHCP
options are a tag, a length and a value, except Pad (0) and End (255),
which are one byte each. Saying so was refused since the tlv grammar was
first checked, on the reason that "a walk over zero-extent values does
not advance" -- which is not so: every item has a tag, and the tag moves
the walk. The note beside it offered "`0` on the tag type" as the
spelling, which names nothing the grammar has.

TCP's options, the same shape, went to a run of records with arms
instead (`example/tcp`), which is right for TCP and is no reason to refuse
the TLV form.

With the refusal lifted, the sweep that found it -- `u8` and `u16` tags,
four value-size rules, alone and behind a sized member -- ran its four
refused shapes, 31 draws each, through the four backends and both walkers
with no disagreement, and the dissector agreed with the walker on one of
them. A message counted by hand, `02 02 01 aa bb 05 01 cc`, is four items
in C. `test_a_tag_may_carry_no_value.py` holds a DHCP-shaped region in all
four backends and both walkers, counts worked out by hand;
`test_tlv.py`'s refusal test is an acceptance test now.

The other twelve TLV shapes in the sweep agreed in all six readers.
