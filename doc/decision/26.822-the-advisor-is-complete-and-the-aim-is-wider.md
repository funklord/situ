## Which of the uncovered ones are advisable at all

**Not all of them, and that matters more than the count.** A cause is
advisable only where a schema change exists that would restore the
capability without describing a different format:

- **Advisable, and these are the three with a population.** A bit-packed
  field loses `atomic` because a write is a read-modify-write of the
  containing byte, and giving the field a whole byte restores it at a
  cost in padding bits -- 4 instances, plus the 1 crossing a byte
  boundary, which is the same family. A run that ends after the element
  failing a condition has `access = Sequential` because nothing says how
  many elements there are, and a count field restores `Random` for one
  or two bytes -- 3 instances. A member whose length comes from an
  earlier field is `mutate = Shifting`, and `must_eq` on the length pins
  it -- 2 instances.
- **Not advisable, because the advice would be "describe a different
  protocol".** Case-insensitive comparison, optional whitespace, an
  `endian_marker`, a delimiter that may occur in content: each is a fact
  about a format situ is describing rather than designing, and section 2
  is where the non-goals live.
- **Not advisable, because the capability is the point.** `tlv` with
  `unknown = preserve` exists to be extensible; advising it away is
  advising against the construct.

So the honest shape of the gap is **three candidate rows, 9 instances in
the corpus**, and not twenty-two.

## Whose decision, and what it costs

**The holder's.** Adding a row to 18.2 is a change to what the product
is, not a defect to fix, and `working-practice.md` asks that a record
describing something not mine to decide name three things: the option,
its cost, and whose it is.

- The option: three rows in 18.2, implemented as three rules in
  `advise.py`'s catalog.
- The cost: each rule is a `find` function in the shape of the eight
  existing ones, plus a row in the spec table and the snapshot a
  suggestion's wording is held to (section 17 makes message quality the
  product, so each has a snapshot test). The two bit-field rows are one
  family and would be one rule.
- Whose: yours.

**And the measurement is the part that keeps.** Whether or not any row is
added, "eight rules against thirty causes" was not knowable from either
section, and the three with a population are the only ones worth
arguing about.

- All thirteen roadmap phases are delivered, and 26.14 records the work
