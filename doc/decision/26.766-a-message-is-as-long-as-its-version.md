# 26.766: A message is as long as its version

Status: recorded -- fixed
Date: 2026-10-30
Source: `project.md` section 26

Section 19.4: "the struct's extent is a range. `size=3..9`: v1 is three
bytes, v3 is nine." Every reader answered nine, whatever the message's own
version said:

    struct versioned [version = ver] {
        u8 ver; u16 length; u32 flags [since = 2]; u16 extra [since = 3];
    }

    01 00 00 | ee ee ...      required() 9 in all four, walkers' extent 9

So a stream of version-1 messages was framed nine bytes at a time, each
swallowing the first six bytes of the next, and a member after a nested
version-1 struct was read from where a version-3 one ends -- `after`
unreadable in all four, an extent of 9 claimed over three bytes. All six
agreed, which is why nothing compared them into noticing; 19.4's own
sentence is the witness.

## What changed

`traverse.version_steps` gives a struct's length at each version: the
members every version carries, then one `(since, end)` step per version
that adds members. Only where that is the whole answer -- every own member
at a static byte offset and of one size, the versioned ones a tail with
nothing unversioned after them, and no `[since]` below an own member --
which is the shape 19.4 describes. Anything else keeps its old answer.

- All four backends' `required` read the version field once the members
  every version carries have arrived, and answer the version's length.
- All four backends' extent does the same, behind the floor every extent
  has, so a nested versioned struct and a run of them are measured by
  their own versions.
- Both walkers' `struct_extent` leave out a `[since]` member the message's
  version does not reach, where everything after it is versioned too.

Over the corpus, `edges.versioned` and `edges.constrained` change their
`required` in all four backends and nothing else changes; no packed image
moves.

## Tests

`test_a_message_is_as_long_as_its_version.py`: the four backends frame a
version-1, -2 and -3 message at 3, 7 and 9 with more bytes following,
place `after` past a nested version-1 and version-3 struct, and frame a
run of one of each at 14; both walkers place `after` alike. With the
changes reverted the backends' cases and the walkers' version-1 and -2
cases fail.

## Still open

A versioned member inside a region -- `authenticated part { u8 a; u8 b
[since = 2]; }` after its tag -- is outside `version_steps`, and its
struct still frames at the newest version's length (26.765's note).
