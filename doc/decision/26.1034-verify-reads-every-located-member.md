# 26.1034: verify reads every located member

Status: recorded
Date: 2026-11-14
Source: openmlx4, inbox 20261114T0025Z (`suggestion/openmlx4.md`
2026-11-14), measured against 73e8e79a

**`situc verify` passed a member placed by `at` far outside the vector.**
openmlx4's MFA TOC entry places its data at a 48-bit offset, low half at
byte 0 and high half at byte 20; with the high half 1, the offset is
0x1_0000_0018 into 26 bytes, and verify said "1 vectors conform" while the
generated accessor refused the same bytes with a BoundsError. A vacuous
pass, in the gate an adopter builds on.

**verify now reads each located member through the reader's own
accessor** once `validate` has accepted the vector, and the accessor's
refusal refuses the vector. A located member with no accessor is named in
the report, as an unchecked tag is (26.1023), rather than counted.

**Measured**: the reproduction is refused with the accessor's message and
the same entry with the high half zero conforms; every committed corpus
still conforms, BMP's located pixels among them.

**Open, and wider than verify.** The out-of-bounds vector is `0` (valid)
to C's `validate`, to Python's, and to the Python walker alike: no reader
and no walker refuses an unconstrained located member the message does not
reach, though 26.967 describes the walkers as refusing "a member the
message does not reach". They agree, so the differential cannot see it.
Making `validate` refuse it is a change of verdict in six places and is
left for its own decision; verify no longer depends on it.
