# 26.952: an early answer owes what follows

Status: recorded
Date: 2026-11-10
Source: 26.946's `READS_LATE`; parked by the run that wrote 26.950
Supersedes: 26.946's netlink and `edges.text_driver_moved` entries under *What is left*

**Every answer `required` gives before the end owes the floors of the
variable members after the point it stops, and a member still arriving
owes one byte past what is here on top of everything else.** Two changes,
both in all four backends.

## The floors of what follows

`at` opens as every fixed member, those after the member being measured
included, so an early answer already owed them. It did not owe the
variable members after it: netlink's message was told 16 below six bytes
and 20 once its type arrived, its `while` run of attributes owing an
element whatever the arm. The last run held this open because owing a
later member's floor from an earlier guard looked like it needed every
early return between them to owe it too, or an answer could fall.

It needs nothing of the kind. `at` now opens with every variable member's
`nested_floor` as well, so every early return in the function owes them
without being touched, and each member gives its own back -- `at -=
floor` -- the moment it is reached, before its length goes in. A length
is never below its floor, so the total never falls; the walks that credit
an element's floor themselves find it already removed. At `UINT32_MAX`
the subtraction is skipped, a saturated need being one no caller can
meet and so one that must not shrink.

## One byte past what is here, on top

A varint or a delimited member not yet whole answered `have + 1`, floored
at `at`. Those are two quantities and the larger is not the sum:
`edges.text_driver_moved` is a varint and then six fixed bytes, and was
told 9 at eight bytes of continuation and 15 at nine whatever the ninth
held. The member runs from the cursor, so it is at least `have - cursor
+ 1` bytes, and the answer is `at` plus that. `lone`, a varint and one
byte, is told 3 from its first byte where it was told 2: the varint is at
least two bytes and the byte after it is owed as well.

## What it took off, and what is left

`edges.text_driver_moved` leaves `READS_LATE`; netlink stays, on a shape
no floor reaches: its discriminant is two bytes in host order, and with a
first byte no named type has, the arm is `default` -- sized by
`nlmsg_len` -- before the second byte arrives. Seven entries are left:
flac's two, netlink, SQLite's leaf cell, and `edges`' `chains`,
`hands_on` and `blocked_arg`, whose nested structs take arguments their
floor does not see.

## How it is held

The readability sweep fails by name for a struct leaving the list or
joining it. `test_a_while_run_owes_an_element` holds netlink at 20 at every
length from zero, and fails with the opening floors removed.
`test_four_backends_frame_alike`, the every-prefix sweeps (never above the
complete answer, never falling) and the exact-size ASan sweeps pass over
the corpus in all four.
