# 26.778: A varint must be whole

Status: recorded -- fixed
Date: 2026-10-31
Source: `project.md` section 26 (the lens 26.776 left)

    struct vint { u8 k; lv v; }      lv: leb128, at most five bytes

A varint's length is in its own bytes, so it declares none `_fits_check`
can hold against the frame, and at a constant offset it was not asked
about at all -- the shape of 26.776 and 26.777 once more. `01 80`, a
continuation byte and then the end, validated clean in all four while
`required` asked for a third byte.

Each backend's `_fits_check` now asks `_varint_whole`: the member's
`_len`, which answers zero for a varint it cannot read whole -- cut
short, or running past its `max_bytes` -- must not be zero. A run of
varints has its own test (26.742) and is not asked.

The corpus changes where a struct holds a single varint: `mqtt`'s
`packet.length`, `sqlite`'s `table_leaf_cell`, and six `edges` structs.

## Tests

`test_a_varint_must_be_whole.py`: the four refuse a varint cut short and
one past its five bytes, and accept whole ones. With the change reverted
both refusals validate.
