# 26.1104: the C walker is handed an argued struct's arguments too

Status: recorded
Date: 2026-11-19
Source: 26.1103's open half -- the two walkers' corpus comparisons skipped
every struct taking a `parameter` (`Unsupplied`)

**Both walker-against-walker sweeps skipped argued structs**, the verdict
comparison and the one naming which check refused, because the C walk's test
driver took no arguments and neither walk may guess one (0050). The
comment at the skip called handing them over its own piece of work: the
two sides would have to agree on how an argument is passed.

## The change

The driver takes the arguments as trailing argv, after the shape, and binds
them with `situ_walk_acquire` before it walks. `argued_by_index` gives each
struct the values the backends' drivers hand it (`differ.argued`, 26.1051),
by its index in the image, and both sweeps hand the same ones to both walks.

Measured over 40 draws per argued struct: 249 refusals compared in `edges`
and 120 in `dnsname`, every one naming the same check in both walks; `mqtt`'s
one argued struct drew no refusal. Before, none of the 369 was compared.
