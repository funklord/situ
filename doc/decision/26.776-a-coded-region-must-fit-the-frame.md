# 26.776: A coded region must fit the frame

Status: recorded -- fixed
Date: 2026-10-31
Source: `project.md` section 26 (found probing 26.774)

    struct codedw { u8 n; coded body(doubling) { u8 content[n]; } }

A coded or sealed region declares no length of its own: its extent is the
interior put through the codec's expansion. `_fits_check` asks about a
member that declares a length and about one at a dynamic offset, and in
all four backends the region was neither, so nothing held it to the
frame. `n = 2` promises a four-byte body; a message carrying three
validated clean in all four -- the accessor clamped it to three -- while
a counted run `u8 d[n]` in the same place has always been refused.
Where something followed the region, that member's own check usually
caught the shortfall; where the region ended the struct, nothing did.

Each backend's `_fits_check` now asks `_region_fits`: the region's
offset, and its extent from the same `_region_length` the accessor uses
before clamping, must fit. A delimited region is measured by its scan
and a fixed one by the struct's minimum, so neither is asked.

The corpus changes where it has such regions -- `dtls.record`,
`keystore`, `packet`, and `edges`' `coded_run` and `sealed_run` -- by
the new check. Because the
region is now a member with a check, `check`'s identifiers after it move
up by one in those structs (`SITU_KEYSTORE_TAG_CHECK` is 7, was 6).

## Tests

`test_a_coded_region_must_fit_the_frame.py`: the four refuse a body
shorter than the expansion promises and accept a whole one, for a plain
region and for 26.774's versioned one. With the change reverted the
short cases validate.
