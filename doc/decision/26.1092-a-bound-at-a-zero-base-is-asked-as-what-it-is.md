# 26.1092: a bound at a zero base is asked as what it is

Status: recorded
Date: 2026-11-18
Source: the cppcheck lens 26.1074 opened, its second shape

**Two more spellings an analyser reads as an unsigned compared with zero.**

- A varint getter at offset zero asked `if (at >= view.limit)` with `at`
  zero: a real question -- does the frame hold a byte at all -- in the form
  `0 >= limit`, which cppcheck calls checking an unsigned for less than
  zero. It is `view.limit == 0u` now, in C's `_get` and `_len` and C++'s.
- `required`'s clamp `*need < at`, before the first variable member where
  the opening total less that member's floor is zero, can never be true:
  26.1074's shape again, in the varint and delimiter branches. It is not
  emitted there, in C or C++.
