# 26.978: the dissector shows a located struct's fields

Status: recorded
Date: 2026-11-12
Source: walking the goal gap -- a TIFF directory in Wireshark

A struct placed by `at` -- a TIFF's first directory at `ifd_offset` --
reached Wireshark as one row of hex. A nested member is handed to its
struct's own dissector; a located one never was, so nothing in the
directory was shown: not its count, not one entry.

It is now: under the bytes' row, at the offset the message gives, with
the member's arguments handed as a nested member's are (0068) -- the
directory's entries read in the header's byte order. Real tshark shows
a big-endian file's fifteen entries with their tags, 256 to 319, and
`test_the_dissector_shows_the_directory_where_it_is` holds the count and
two tags laid out by hand, failing with the change reverted.
