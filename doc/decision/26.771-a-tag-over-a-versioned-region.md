# 26.771: A tag over a versioned region covers its version

Status: recorded -- fixed
Date: 2026-10-30
Source: `project.md` section 26

    struct sealeds [version = ver] {
        u8 ver;
        tag u8 mac[4] covers(part);
        authenticated part { u8 a; u16 b [since = 2]; }
    }

A region's placement is its newest version's size. Every backend's
`_region_end` took the end from it for a fixed region at a static offset,
so a whole six-byte version-1 message -- one byte of `part` -- was told
its tag covers bytes 5..8, past the frame. All four refused to say what
the tag covers, so the tag of an older message could not be computed or
checked at all. With `d[n]` in front of the tag the region's end came
from its last member or the view's end, and the four disagreed: C one
byte, the others two or four. And C's `validate` refused the whole
version-1 message over the region's own frame check, taken at the
newest size; the other three ask no such check of an `authenticated`
region.

`traverse.versioned_region` gives a fixed-size region holding `[since]`
members its size at the oldest version and at each version that adds to
it. All four `_region_end` add that to the region's start, read through
the version field. C's `validate` leaves out the region's own frame check
for such a region; its members are checked one by one, the versioned
ones inside their version guard.

Found by a four-way random comparison over `[since]` members behind a
variable member, which 26.770's lens produced; the static shape failed
the same way in all four, so the comparison alone could not see it. The
corpus has no such tag and generates byte-identically.

## Tests

`test_a_tag_over_a_versioned_region.py`: the four cover a whole
version-1, a short version-2 and a whole version-2 message at 5+1,
refused and 5+3, and the same behind `d[n]` at 6+1, refused and 7+3,
with `validate` agreeing. With the change reverted the first case is
refused.
