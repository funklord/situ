# 26.774: A coded region is as long as its version

Status: recorded -- fixed
Date: 2026-10-31
Source: `project.md` section 26 (the open register entry 26.773 left)

    coded body(doubling) { u8 content[n]; u16 x [since = 2]; }

`traverse.region_extent` counted every interior member, so a version-1
message, which carries no `x`, was given a body two interior bytes longer
than it holds -- four on the wire under `ratio_exact(2, 1)` -- in all four
backends and in the walkers' size program. The layout's minimum counted
`x` as well, so a whole version-1 message with an empty body was refused
as short and the drivers printed nothing for it.

`region_extent` now takes a `[since]` tail of the interior out of
`constant` and returns it as `version` and per-version `steps`; it
declines a versioned interior that is not a tail of fixed members, or
whose version field is not at a constant byte offset, rather than count
what may not be there. The four backends add `(version >= since) ?
bytes : 0` per step to the interior before the expansion, and `pack`
emits the same as `FIELD`, `GE`, `MUL` for both walkers. The layout's
region minimum leaves the interior's `[since]` members out, as
`_versioned_tail_bits` does for a struct's own tail.

The corpus has no such region and generates byte-identically.

## Tests

`test_a_coded_region_is_as_long_as_its_version.py`: the four agree on a
body of 0, 2, 4 and 6 bytes for version 1 and 2 with `n` of 0 and 1, and
both walkers measure the same. With `situc` reverted the four-way case
and the walkers' version-1 cases fail; with only `pack.py` reverted, the
walkers' do.
