# 26.940: the dissector renders a size's bit operators from its tree

Status: recorded
Date: 2026-11-08
Source: `id3_tag.frames`, the last ID3 decline

**An expression whose text holds an operator decision 0021 keeps out of the dissector is
rendered from its tree** where its arithmetic can be shown equal: a shift by a constant
is a division or a product, a mask of contiguous bits a difference of remainders, `|` a
sum where the two sides cannot share a bit, and `/` a floored division where neither
side can be negative. Which bits a side can set comes from the fields' widths. Anything
else -- `^`, a mask with holes, a shift by a variable, a quotient that may be negative --
is still declined.

ID3's frame size is synchsafe, `(size_0 << 21) | (size_1 << 14) | (size_2 << 7) |
size_3` over four `u7`s, so every frame's content was declined and a tag showed its
header and nothing after. `n / 2 + 1`, which the dissector had declined since Lua's `/`
divides as a float, is `math.floor(n / 2) + 1` now.

A tlv region's parts are given widths the same way -- the tag its type's, each part what
its own expression can set -- since CoAP's escape is `(tag >> 4) / 13` and the division
guard declined it until they were.

Held to mutagen, which nothing in situ wrote, on the tag `id3.vectors` takes from a real
MP3: the same nine frames in the same order. The test fails with `|` declined.
