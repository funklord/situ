# 26.933: a dissector arm is read as its member would be

Status: recorded
Date: 2026-11-08
Source: radiotap in real tshark, beside Wireshark's own radiotap dissector

**An arm that is only an `[align]` pad has a length here**: to the next multiple of its
unit from the start of the struct, as the body's own pad counts from the start of the
struct's tvb. It had none, so the variant holding it stopped the cursor, and radiotap
showed no field after its presence words.

Reaching the arms showed two more faults in them:

- **Every arm was added big-endian.** A member outside an arm uses `add_le` where it is
  little-endian and an arm never did, so `mactime` and `lock_quality` read
  byte-reversed: 0x0807060504030201 where Wireshark reads 0x0102030405060708.
- **An arm that is one struct was stepped over and not shown**, the comment saying its
  own Proto shows it when nothing called that Proto. It goes to its dissector now, as a
  nested member does, so radiotap's channel frequency appears.

Held to Wireshark's built-in radiotap dissector, which nothing in situ wrote, over every
committed vector: mactime, channel frequency, signal and lock quality agree row for
row, and the test fails with `add_le` removed. Rate is left out, Wireshark's datarate
being computed from MCS and VHT as well as the rate byte.
