# 26.1020: the other three write an argued arm

Status: recorded
Date: 2026-11-14
Source: 26.1012, which was C's alone

**26.1012 let C write an arm whose extent is its argument** -- DNS's
RDATA, wasm's section bodies: the caller's bytes are one of the arm
exactly when they are as long as the length field the argument names, and
their content is validated with the message. Python, C++ and Rust refused
every such arm, as a nested member taking arguments.

**Now all four**, the same way: the bytes' length against the field's
value, CONSTRAINT where they differ, the content left to the closing
validation. Only where the arm has no peeked discriminant to check as
well; an argued nested member that is not such an arm stays refused.

**Measured** by the agreement test, which now builds DNS's NS, CNAME and
other-type records and wasm's custom, type, function, code, data and other
sections alike in all four, read whole by the reader and the walker; and
RFC 1035's A record for example.com from Python's builder, byte for byte
C's, with RDLENGTH 5 over four bytes refused.
