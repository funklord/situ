# 26.1023: verify checks checksums

Status: recorded
Date: 2026-11-14
Source: the copyright holder, asking why `situc verify` does not verify
checksums and whether it should

**It did not, and a wrong CRC conformed.** Measured: PNG's IEND chunk with
its CRC's last byte changed, `...AE426083`, reported "2 vectors conform"
beside the correct `...AE426082`.

**Why, and why that was not a reason.** `verify` holds a vector to the
generated `validate`, and `validate` deliberately leaves checksums out:
their coverage can run to the end of the message, which is not the flat
walk 0051 settled on, and the generated header says to check them "where
the caller has the whole message" (26.622). `verify` is that caller --
every vector is in memory whole -- and its purpose is to hold a schema to
bytes another implementation wrote, where a CRC it got wrong is exactly
the finding a corpus exists for. It had simply never called the check.

**Now it does.** After `validate`, every tag of the vector's struct that a
codec computes has its `_check` called, and a disagreement refuses the
vector with the stored and computed values. A tag it cannot check is named
in the report as unchecked, and the summary says "except unchecked tags"
rather than counting it a pass: no codec (DTLS's AEAD tag, which needs a
key), a sum over a pseudo-header the vector does not carry (UDP's), or a
tag inside a nested member, which this does not descend into yet.

**Measured**: PNG's IEND and a FLAC frame header from the committed corpus
each conform with their real CRC and are refused, `ChecksumError`, with one
byte of it changed; every committed corpus still conforms, the FLAC frame
headers now with their CRC-8s checked; DTLS's record is reported with its
tag unchecked.
