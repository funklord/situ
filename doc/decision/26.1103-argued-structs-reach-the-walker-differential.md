# 26.1103: argued structs reach the walker differential

Status: recorded
Date: 2026-11-19
Source: 0050's open item -- a struct taking a `parameter` was compared by
the four backends (with invented arguments, 26.1051) and by no walker

**Every argued struct was dropped from the walker-against-C comparison.**
The drivers hand each one invented arguments (`differ.argued`: its `[min]`,
else 1, held under its `[max]`) and print it under `-- argued <name>`; the
walker's `report.listing` takes one argument list for the whole schema, the
differential handed none, so the walk answered `needs-arguments` for each
and the comparison, which intersects the two sides' keys, kept nothing.
Measured on one buffer: 18 argued structs across `edges`, `mqtt` and
`dnsname`, 0 keys compared.

## The change

`listing` takes `argued`, a struct's own arguments by name, over the shared
list; the differential hands it the drivers' values and keys the walk's
lines under the drivers' `argued` section. The same buffer then compares 56
of the drivers' 59 keys -- the three left are members the walker does not
list.

It found no disagreement: the Python walker agrees with C about every argued
struct in the eight schemas that declare one. That is the finding, and a
differential that could not see these structs could not have said so.

## Still open

The C walker's corpus comparison skips the same structs (`Unsupplied`),
because its driver would have to take arguments as trailing argv, as the
backends' does. Recorded in `test_walker_c` where it skips.
