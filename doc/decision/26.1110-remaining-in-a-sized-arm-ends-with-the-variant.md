# 26.1110: `[remaining]` in a sized arm ends with the variant, whatever follows it

Status: recorded
Date: 2026-11-20
Source: met writing 26.1109's test; the case openmlx4's MFA MAP and VPD
keyword sections need, a run of records inside a section that has
something after it

`variant body[len] switch (...) { default: rec r[remaining]; }` followed by
any member was refused by `layout`: "`[remaining]` must be the last member
of its frame". 26.1040 made a variant that states its size its arms'
frame -- `remaining` inside it ends where the variant does -- but
`place_variant` passed the arm the *variant's* position in the struct, so a
member after the variant read as a member after the run.

## The change

An arm of a variant that states its size is placed as last in its frame.
Nothing else moves: the member after the variant is placed at the
variant's offset plus its stated size, as it already was, and 26.1109
already ends each reader's walk of the arm's run at the variant.

Test: the `tailed` rows of
`test_a_run_in_a_sized_arm_ends_with_the_variant.py` -- six readers,
verdicts worked by hand: a whole body, three records with the last empty,
a record past the body, and the tail's own `must_eq`.
