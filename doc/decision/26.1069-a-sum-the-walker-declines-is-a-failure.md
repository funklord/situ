# 26.1069: a sum the walker declines is a failure, and FLAC's frame CRC is summed

Status: recorded
Date: 2026-11-18
Source: the lens 26.1067 left -- a comparison over the keys both sides print
drops a verdict one side does not print

**The walker never summed FLAC's frame CRC, and nothing said so.**
`test_the_walker_agrees_with_the_compiled_backends` compares the walker's
listing with C's driver over the keys both print, which is right for
members: the differ asks about a subset of them, so a member one side
answers and the other was never asked is a difference in the question. A
sum is not like that. The differ asks `SUM` only where every reader
computes the tag, so a sum C answers -- a value or `refused` -- and the
walker does not is a sum the walker declined. TCP's and UDP's were
dropped that way until 26.1066, and the dissector's refusals until 26.1067.

Asked, the corpus had one more: `flac.frame_header.crc`. C printed
`crc.sum 219` on a random draw and the walker printed nothing, on that
draw and on a valid frame (`fff86408000fce`, CRC-8 0xce) alike.

**The cause was a stale filter.** `report._offset_computable` called any
member after one of no fixed size unplaceable unless that member was a
varint or delimited -- a list written before the walk measured nested
structs by their extent. `frame_header` is `authenticated summed {
frame_fields fields; }` then `crc`, and `fields` is variable-length (its
UTF-8-coded frame number), so the CRC was never listed, neither its
`present=` line nor its sum. `walk.offset_bits` places it correctly (6 on
the valid frame) and the C walker sums it (206).

## The rule

- One nested struct -- a field, not a run, not delimited -- no longer makes
  the members after it unplaceable: the walk measures it, as `_nested`
  already did to answer `ok= extent=`.
- The walker sweep fails where C answers a `.sum` the walker does not.

## Measured

Before the fix the new assertion failed on `flac` only, of 60 schemas
(`frame_header.crc.sum`); after it all 60 pass, with FLAC's CRC compared
on every draw. The C walker needed nothing.
