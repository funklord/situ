# 26.1009: a reply is matched without ending it

Status: recorded
Date: 2026-11-13
Source: fuzznet, `suggestion/fuzznet.md` 2026-11-13, gap 3

**fuzznet's replies are many datagrams**, and it drops a late piece of a
settled message before reassembly, so a stray cannot take a slot. That
check runs once a piece. Rung 5's `match` (C) and `take` (C++, Rust,
Python), and rung 6's `on_message`, all answer which request a reply
belongs to by ending the exchange, so the question could be asked once, at
the end.

**`peek` now answers it and leaves the exchange pending**, in all four
conversation tables and all four drivers; it is refused, as `match` is,
for a reply nothing is waiting on. Purely additive: no signature moved.

**Measured** in each driver: `peek` names the request's id twice over,
`on_message` still matches afterwards, and `peek` is then refused. C++'s
and Python's tables were compiled and imported beside them.

**fuzznet's other two gaps stay open, deliberately.** Telling `submit`
which exchange it sends (so a resend can be resealed under a fresh nonce)
and saying WHICH exchanges expired both change rung 6's interface in four
languages -- the I/O vtable's signature and `step`'s out-parameter. fuzznet
says it is not blocked, and a signature change is better made when a
consumer is adopting it than ahead of one. Recorded for when fuzznet moves.
