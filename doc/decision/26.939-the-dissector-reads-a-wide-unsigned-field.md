# 26.939: the dissector reads an unsigned field of up to eight bytes

Status: recorded
Date: 2026-11-08
Source: Matroska's `wide_cluster`, the last of that schema's declines

**A field of five to eight bytes is read as two halves and arithmetic**, `hi * 2^32 +
lo` in the field's byte order. Wireshark's `uint` stops at four bytes, and the
dissector refused anything wider -- "no length field needs it". A discriminant does:
`wide_cluster` peeks a `u64` to tell the unknown size, 0x01FFFFFFFFFFFFFF, from a known
one, and its variant was declined, so a streamed WebM file showed its header and
nothing inside a Cluster. The read is exact as a Lua 5.4 integer and as near as a float
gets in 5.2, which is Wireshark's older Lua. A signed field that wide is still declined.

Held to ffprobe on the GStreamer `streamed` vector: the dissector consumes all 547 bytes,
and every SimpleBlock it shows is a packet ffprobe reads, at the same place and of the
same size; the test fails with the wide read refused.
