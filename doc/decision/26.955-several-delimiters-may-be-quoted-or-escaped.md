# 26.955: several delimiters may be quoted or escaped

Status: recorded
Date: 2026-11-11
Source: a sweep of delimited runs through six readers, and 26.290's refusal
Supersedes: 26.290's refusal of "a delimited member with several [delimiters] and an escaping form"

**`u8 field[] until "," | "\r\n" [quoted = "\""]` is accepted and read
alike by every reader.** A CSV field ends at a comma or at the line's
end and may be quoted; an HTTP parameter ends at `;` or CRLF and may be a
quoted-string. 26.290 refused several delimiters beside `quoted` or
`escape` because the quote's state would have to be carried past each
alternative, "a fifth scan rather than a parameter".

## What it took

The fifth scan: `situ_scan_any_relaxed` in C (C++ shares it),
`scan_any_relaxed` in Rust and Python, each `scan_any`'s earliest-first,
longest-second loop with `scan_relaxed`'s one quote flag and its escape
skip -- the same loop, byte for byte. The four generators call it where
the member has both. **Neither walker needed anything**: both had always
scanned several alternatives under one quote state, and so had the
dissector. The language refused what only the backends lacked.

## What stays refused

A run of RECORDS with several terminators and an escaping form. It
checks its terminators where an element would start (26.930) and keeps no
quote's state between elements, so the refusal stays, narrowed to that
case, and `test_characters.py` holds it there.

## How it is held

`test_several_delimiters_may_be_escaped.py`, with lengths computed by hand
rather than taken from any reader: a bare comma, a bare CRLF, a quoted
comma, a quoted CRLF, and two quoted runs in one field, through all four
backends and both walkers. It fails with C's call put back to the plain
`situ_scan_any`. The sweep that found the gap -- one, two and alternative
delimiters, with and without a cap, plain, trimmed, quoted, escaped and
case-insensitive, 30 draws each through six readers -- had 26 accepted
shapes and no disagreement. The shapes this record admits were then run
the same way: `,` or `;` and `,` or CRLF, capped and not, quoted and
escaped -- eight shapes, 60 draws each, and no disagreement.
