# 26.769: The dissector frames by version

Status: recorded -- fixed
Date: 2026-10-30
Source: `project.md` section 26

26.766 and 26.768 gave the four backends a versioned struct's own
version's length; 26.768 recorded the Wireshark dissector as not done.
Its extent functions summed every member, so `edges.versioned` answered
nine bytes and `edges.constrained` five whatever the message's version,
and `varv { u8 ver; u8 n; u8 d[n]; u16 x [since = 2]; }` three more than
its count. A version-1 struct nested in another overran the packet and
the dissector showed nothing at all.

Now `_versioned_constant` renders `traverse.version_steps` as a chain on
the version, read through `situ_uint` (zero where the frame is too short,
which is the oldest length), and `_extent_steps` leaves a
`traverse.versioned_tail` out of the sum and adds it by version. A version
field in host order is unreadable from a capture and keeps the old sum.

Over the committed schemas only `edges.lua` changes, in those two
structs' extents. `test_a_nested_struct_is_as_long_as_its_version`
places `outer.after` behind a nested `fixed` and `varv` of versions 1, 2
and 3; with the change reverted all three consume past the packet.

A counted run of a versioned struct is still declined ("elements of no
fixed size"), before and after.
