# 26.773: A ratio region expands its fixed bytes too

Status: recorded -- fixed
Date: 2026-10-30
Source: `project.md` section 26

    coded body(doubling) { u8 content[n]; u16 x; }    ratio_exact(2, 1)

The interior is `n + 2` bytes and the region `2 * (n + 2)` on the wire.
C++ and Rust rendered `traverse.region_extent`'s `ratio` rule as
`(wide * out) / into` with `wide` a sum, so the product bound to its
last term: `2 + n * 2`, two bytes short of C's and Python's
`((wide) * out) / into`, and every member after the region moved. The
corpus's only ratio region, `edges.coded_run`, has nothing fixed in its
interior; its constant is zero, the two readings are equal, and the
four-way differential had nothing to see.

Both now parenthesise the sum. The `add` and `padded` forms only add
around it and were right. Over the committed schemas only `edges.hpp`
and `edges.rs` change, by the parentheses, with the same values.

Found by a four-way random comparison over a coded region holding a
`[since]` member; the fault is not about versions, and the same region
without `[since]` disagreed the same way.

## Tests

`test_a_ratio_region_doubles_its_constant.py`: the four agree that the
region is 4, 6 and 10 bytes for `n` = 0, 1 and 3. With the change
reverted they disagree.
