# 26.1070: an authenticated region hides nothing from the walker's listing

Status: recorded
Date: 2026-11-18
Source: a corpus measurement of the lines C's driver answers and the
walker's listing omits, taken after 26.1069

**Five of the walker's six member lists left out anything inside any
region.** `report._scalars` had learned the distinction long ago: a member
behind a `sealed` region's gate is read through the gate, while one in an
`authenticated` region has no gate and is read where it sits -- the
sentence in its comment says skipping those left every field of ICMP's
message out. The other lists -- nested structs, byte runs, delimited
members, sized runs of records, delimited runs -- still asked `index in
image.regions`, which is true of both.

So wherever a checksum covered a region, the nested structs and runs in it
were never compared: IPv4's `source`, `destination` and `options`, TCP's
`options` and `payload`, UDP's `payload`, ICMP's `rest`, PNG's `kind` and
`data`, FLAC's `fields`, and eight more in `packet` and `edges`. Every
backend answered each; the sweep compares only lines both print, so none
was missing from any test.

## The rule

`report._behind_gate` is the one predicate, `_scalars`' own: a member is
left out only where its region is sealed. Every list asks it.

## Measured

Of the lines C answers that the walker omitted over the corpus, 18
`ok=`/`len=` lines are compared now, all agreeing with C over 60 schemas.
What is left of that measurement, and is not this record's: 350
`required()` lines (the walker answers none), 30 `.covered` lines, fixed
text numbers (`ok=1 value=`, cpio's thirteen), and variant-arm members.
